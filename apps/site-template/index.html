How does the free clinic evaluation work?

Your initial symptom review carries no consultation fee; subsequent diagnostics or follow-ups are quoted clearly before consent.

Are Noida video prescriptions pharmacy-valid?

Yes. Every consultation includes a signed digital prescription under India Telemedicine Guidelines with NMC registration details.

How is patient record privacy protected?

Contact and clinical records are encrypted and processed strictly under India DPDP Act 2023 consent controls.

Should acute chest pain use this booking desk?

No. For sudden chest pain, breathing distress, or stroke warning signs, call 112 or visit the nearest hospital ER immediately.
