Frequently Asked Questions

Common Patient Enquiries

Quick answers about consultation hours, fees, diagnostic tests, and appointments.

Do I need a prior appointment before visiting?

Walk-ins are welcomed during all clinic hours. However, messaging on WhatsApp or calling ahead helps assign an estimated consultation window, minimizing your waiting room time.

What is the consultation fee and follow-up policy?

The standard consultation fee is ₹600. All patients are eligible for one complimentary follow-up visit within 7 days of the initial consultation for review of lab reports or treatment progress.

Is home blood sample collection available?

Yes, our clinic partners with certified NABL diagnostic laboratory partners. You can schedule a phlebotomist visit for home sample collection by informing clinic reception.

What payment modes are accepted at the clinic?

We accept all digital payment options including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), debit/credit cards, and cash.

How do I receive my prescription and reports?

You receive both a printed hard copy and an official verified digital prescription delivered directly to your WhatsApp number immediately following the consultation.

Can I consult Dr. Maanvita over WhatsApp for follow-ups?

Yes! Existing patients can send their lab test reports and quick dosage follow-up queries to the clinic WhatsApp number during clinic working hours.

Are medical fitness and sick leave certificates provided?

Yes, official verified medical fitness certificates and sick leave verification are issued following physical clinical assessment, stamped with DMC registration credentials.

What emergency facilities are available?

The clinic provides primary acute emergency stabilization (oxygen, nebulization, primary wound dressing, glucose revival). Severe trauma or cardiac emergencies are stabilized and referred to partner tertiary hospitals.
